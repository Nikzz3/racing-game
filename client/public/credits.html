Vehicles

  • Generic passenger car pack by Comrade1280. Source files distributed by TUM-VT / Sumonity. Used for the sports coupé, hatchback, 4×4, taxi, police utility and van. Changes: PBR materials, map conversion, wheel textures, game axes and wheel pivots, separate lamps, taxi markings and roof sign, police markings, lightbar and push bar.
  • Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group, © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, based on Unity Fan's public-domain concept. Used for the track car. Changes: orange paint, reflective glass and adjusted wheels, rear wing, reduced geometry, game pivots and material batching, and reshaped headlight openings. Khronos logos removed.
  • SportsCar by Yasutoshi Mori, © 2015. Used for the hypercar and the track car's adapted projector headlight assemblies. Changes: subdivision and geometry reduction, removal of hidden mechanical assemblies and badges, cyan PBR paint and material conversion, animated wheel pivots and material batching.
